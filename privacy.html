Privacy Policy — Sky Dash

Last updated: 2026

Sky Dash ("the Game") is a casual arcade game. This page explains what information the Game collects and how it is used.

Information the Game Stores

The Game stores your best score, chosen skin, difficulty, sound/vibration preference, and an optional player name you enter, on your own device (local storage). This information is not sent anywhere unless the optional online leaderboard feature is enabled, in which case your chosen name and score are stored so other players can see the leaderboard.

Advertising

The Game may display ads served through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to show relevant ads, in accordance with Google's own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. You can review Google's ad settings at adssettings.google.com.

Children

The Game does not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what is described above (an optional, self-chosen display name).
